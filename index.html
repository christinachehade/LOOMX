FAQ

Questions we hear before every kickoff.

Do we need to already be "AI-ready" to work with you? +

No. Most clients start with spreadsheets, email, and disconnected tools. Our job is to meet you where you are, find the highest-leverage automation, and build from there — no existing AI infrastructure required.

How long does a typical project take? +

A single automated workflow typically launches within 1–2 weeks of kickoff. Larger, multi-system builds usually run 4–8 weeks, scoped in sprints so you see working pieces along the way, not just a final reveal.

What tools and platforms do you work with? +

We work across your existing stack — common CRMs, help desks, e-commerce platforms, spreadsheets, and databases — connected via Zapier, Make, or custom API integrations, plus leading AI models where they add real value.

Who owns the systems once they're built? +

You do. Every account, integration, and line of documentation is handed over at launch. We're not a black box you're locked into — Growth Partner clients keep us on retainer because they want to, not because they have to.

What does the free audit actually involve? +

A 30-minute call where we walk through your current workflows, flag the biggest time-sinks, and outline what an automated version would look like — plus a rough scope and price. No obligation, no generic pitch deck.

Is our data secure? +

Yes. We follow least-privilege access on every integration, never train external models on your proprietary data without explicit agreement, and can work within your existing compliance requirements for regulated industries.
