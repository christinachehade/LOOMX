FAQ

Questions we hear before every kickoff.

Do we need to already be "AI-ready" to work with you? +

No. Most practices start with paper intake forms, phone-based scheduling, and a PMS that doesn't talk to anything else. Our job is to meet you where you are and automate the highest-leverage bottleneck first — no existing AI infrastructure required.

Will this replace our front-desk staff? +

No — it's built to support them, not replace them. AI agents handle the repetitive first pass (reminders, rescheduling, routine questions) and hand off anything that needs a human judgment call to your team.

Does this work with our EHR or practice management system? +

We integrate with most major EHR and PMS platforms through their existing APIs, plus scheduling, billing, and communication tools. If your system doesn't expose an open API, we'll tell you upfront before any work begins.

How long does a typical project take? +

A single automated workflow typically launches within 1–2 weeks of kickoff. Larger, multi-system builds usually run 4–8 weeks, scoped in sprints so you see working pieces along the way, not just a final reveal.

Who owns the systems once they're built? +

You do. Every account, integration, and line of documentation is handed over at launch. We're not a black box you're locked into — Growth Partner clients keep us on retainer because they want to, not because they have to.

Is our patient data secure? +

Yes. We follow least-privilege access on every integration, never train external models on your patient data, and design every system around your existing compliance requirements — including HIPAA.
