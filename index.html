How We Work

From idea to working product.

Every project starts by understanding the clinical problem, research, users, and opportunity. From there, we shape the right product, design it, build it, and help bring it into the real world.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We learn the medicine, research, users, workflow, and problem behind the idea.

  2. 02

    Shape

    We define what the product should become, who it is for, what belongs in the first version, and what success looks like.

  3. 03

    Design

    We translate the clinical or research concept into clear workflows, prototypes, and an intuitive product experience — then test and refine it before development.

  4. 04

    Build

    We engineer the real product — web, mobile, clinical platforms, integrations, infrastructure, AI capabilities where useful, and everything required to make it work.

  5. 05

    Launch & Evolve

    We help prepare the product for pilots, deployment, real users, and continued iteration — whether the goal is clinical use, research validation, or commercialization.

Already further along? We can enter at any stage; from an early idea or grant proposal to an existing prototype or product that needs to be redesigned, rebuilt, or taken to launch.

FAQ

Questions we hear before every kickoff.

Do you only do AI work, or full product design and development? +

Both, and they are often the same project. We run UX research, design patient and clinician-facing products, build websites, apps, platforms, and dashboards, and integrate AI where it earns its place. Some clients come to us for research alone. Others want the whole thing designed, built, and shipped.

Can you just teach our team how to use AI? +

Yes, and it is one of the most useful places to start. We choose the right tools for your setting, build assistants and prompts around your protocols, and train your staff hands-on, including where AI helps and where clinical judgment stays in charge. Most teams have access to AI but no structured training, which is exactly why adoption stalls.

Do you work with hospitals and health systems, or only smaller practices? +

Both. We have built platforms and clinical tools at health-system scale, and we also set up AI and automate the front desk for single-site clinics. The process scales to the size of the problem.

We have research or a validated model. Can you turn it into a real product? +

Yes. Taking clinical research and validated models from a paper or a prototype into a deployed, usable product is core work for us, covering the design, the interface, the integration, and the rollout.

Do we need to be "AI-ready" to work with you? +

No. Most teams start with paper intake, phone-based scheduling, and systems that do not talk to each other. We meet you where you are and start with the highest-value fix, whether that is research, a design, or a single automation.

Will this replace our staff? +

No. It is built to support them. AI handles the repetitive first pass, such as reminders, rescheduling, and routine questions, and hands anything needing human judgment to your team.

Does this work with our EHR or practice management system? +

We integrate with most major EHR and PMS platforms through their existing APIs and modern standards including FHIR and HL7, plus scheduling, billing, and communication tools. If your system does not expose an open API, we will tell you upfront before any work begins.

How long does a typical project take? +

An AI setup or a single automated workflow typically goes live within 1 to 2 weeks. A research or strategy engagement usually runs 2 to 4 weeks. Design and build projects generally run 4 to 12 weeks, scoped in sprints so you see working pieces along the way.

What does it cost? +

Every engagement is scoped to what you actually need, so we price after the free audit rather than before. You will know the full scope and price before anything is signed.

Who owns the systems once they are built? +

You do. Every account, integration, and line of documentation is handed over at launch, so your team stays in control of what runs your organization.

Is our patient data secure? +

Yes. We follow least-privilege access on every integration, never train external models on your patient data, and design every system around the requirements that apply to you, including HIPAA in the US, PIPEDA in Canada, and GDPR in Europe.
